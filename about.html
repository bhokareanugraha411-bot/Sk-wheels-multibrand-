SK Wheels Multibrand Workshop

SK Wheels is an automobile service and repair workshop near Balaji Mandir on Kuchi Road. We handle wheel alignment, wheel balancing, denting, painting, car washing, mechanical work and spare parts, so you can get several jobs done in one visit.

The workshop has an open service bay with vehicle lifts, alignment equipment, a wash area and a customer office.
